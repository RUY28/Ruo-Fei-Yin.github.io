Hi, I'm RuoFei(April) Yin. I'm an fourth year PhD student in Biostatistics at Graduate Schoole of Public Health, University of Pittsburgh under the guidance of [Dr. George Tseng](https://sites.pitt.edu/~ctseng/). I also worked as a graduate student researcher with [Dr. Colleen McClung](https://www.mcclung.pitt.edu/), [Dr. Marianne Seney](https://www.cnup.pitt.edu/people/marianne-seney) and [Dr. Kyle Ketchesin](https://www.cnup.pitt.edu/people/ant-237) at the Department of Psychiatry, University of Pittsburgh. I received my M.S. degree also in Biostatistics, University of Pittsburgh. 

My research lies at the intersection of statistical methodology and biomedical applications, with the goal of advancing health data science. I develop statistical and machine learning methods for omics and clinical data, including data preprocessing pipeline, mixed-effects and meta-analytic models, Bayesian modeling for data integration and subtype discovery, and power analysis for clinical trial design. I collaborate with researchers in psychiatry, neuroscience, and oncology on circadian rhythms, cell type deconvolution, biomarker discovery, and machine learning for clinical risk prediction.

In summer 2026, I interned at Takeda Pharmaceutical Company as an Oncology Computational Biology Intern, where I worked on Bayesian power and sample-size analysis for oncology trial planning, statistical modeling for companion diagnostic development, and simulation-based evaluation of missing-data imputation methods for biomarker discovery.
